## Overview

My first client website created for my grandmother's business. I am currently in the process of re-making this site with my improved skills and technologies.

## Technologies

- HTML
- CSS
- TailwindCSS

## Key Features

- Landing page
- Tailwind Components
- Dropdowns
- Contact Page

## Challenges

Since this was my first client website, I struggled to get what I needed from my client, and fulfilling their vision was difficult considering so many design elements clashed with what I thought looked best.

## Solution

In the end, we reached mutual understandings and the final product turned out alright. I am hoping to address the major design problems in my next implementation utilizing Svelte and a more comprehensive set of tools.

## Outcome

I was proud of my work at first, but over time and after several more projects, I have realized several areas for improvement that I hope to fix in my next iteration.
